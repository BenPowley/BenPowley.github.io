## About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Linnaeus University, Växjö, Sweden. My research interests are Immersive Analytics, Data Visualization, Human Computer Interaction, and Human Centered Computing. I completed my PhD in Computer Science at Victoria University of Wellington, New Zealand. My thesis is titled "Immersive visualization for ecosystem services analysis and trade-offs". I am passionate about doing interdisciplinary research using complex real world data, including environmental/geospatial data, to make a positive difference to science and decision making.
